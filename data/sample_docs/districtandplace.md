# Districts, Cities and Towns of India: RAG Directory

> Administrative vintage warning: district boundaries and names change. Verify against the National Portal and the relevant state government.
> Coverage warning: district lists are broad current-reference lists. The place field gives representative major cities/towns, not every village or settlement.

# Maharashtra Districts
## Mumbai (city district)
- State: Maharashtra
- District: Mumbai
- Major places: South Mumbai, Central Mumbai, Eastern Suburbs, Western Suburbs
- Economy: Financial services, IT, entertainment industry, trade
- Culture: Capital of Indian cinema, cosmopolitan culture

## Pune
- State: Maharashtra
- District: Pune
- Major places: Pune city, Pimpri-Chinchwad
- Economy: IT hub, auto manufacturing, pharmaceuticals
- Culture: Educational center, classical music traditions, pilgrim destinations

## Nagpur
- State: Maharashtra
- District: Nagpur
- Major places: Nagpur city, Wardha
- Economy: Oranges cultivation, cotton, textiles
- Culture: Geographical center of India, Buddhist heritage
